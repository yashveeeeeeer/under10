Privacy

Files stay in browser memory. No uploads, tracking or document storage. Clear the session to release them; Download saves a copy. The site host may keep visit logs. Your browser and operating system control memory, caches and downloads.

Signed PDFs

Signed files are check-only. Compressing or merging can break a digital signature. Use unsigned originals to prepare a new PDF, then sign it. This tool does not verify signatures.

Choosing a size

Use the limit shown on your upload form. If you plan to sign the PDF later, we aim for 90% of that limit. Check its size again after signing. This tool is not affiliated with MCA.

Supported files

Static unsigned PDFs, JPGs and PNGs. Up to 40 files, 40 MB each, 100 MB total and 200 pages. Forms, annotations, portfolios and embedded attachments are check-only. New PDFs do not retain document-level bookmarks, tags or metadata.
