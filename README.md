# Under10

Less file. Less friction.

Turn your PDFs and images into one PDF under your chosen size limit. Free to use; no account needed.

**[Open Under10 →](https://yashveeeeeeer.github.io/under10/)**

## Drop; prepare; download

Drop your PDFs, JPGs or PNGs; put them in order; choose a limit up to 10 MB.

Click **Prepare PDF**; check every page; download when you're happy. Your original files stay untouched. If the result cannot fit within the quality limits, the tool stops instead of dropping pages.

## Already signed?

Digitally signed PDFs are **not compressed or merged**, even with scan compression switched on. Rewriting them can break their signatures.

Use **Check an existing PDF** to check its size and download it unchanged if it fits. If it's too large, prepare a smaller unsigned original, then sign again. The tool does not verify signature validity.

## Your files stay with you

Documents are processed in temporary browser memory; they are never uploaded or saved by the app unless you choose Download. Clear the session when you're done. Browser and operating-system storage are outside the app's control.

## One last thing

This is an independent beta, not an MCA service. Check your form's actual attachment limit; it may be lower than 10 MB.
