Can every document be made smaller than 10 MB?

No. Some documents cannot fit without losing too much detail. Under10 tries a bounded set of colour image settings, down to 150 DPI and JPEG quality 72%, then stops. These settings are a product floor, not an MCA standard. The tool never silently removes pages or offers an oversized result.

Is 10 MB the rule for every MCA attachment?

No universal limit is assumed. Limits can differ by form, individual attachment and total package. An older SPICe+ FAQ says 6 MB per PDF form; a URC-1 kit says 10 MB total; the RD-1 kit specifies 2 MB per attachment and 10 MB total. Confirm the current requirement in your live filing workflow. This independent utility checks size, not filing eligibility.

What happens to my documents?

File bytes, names and previews stay in this browser session. There is no upload endpoint, analytics, cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB or service worker in the app. Clear session releases its file references and previews; closing or reloading also resets the workspace. Download saves a file only when you choose it.

The site host still receives normal page requests and may keep access logs, such as IP addresses. Browsers and operating systems manage memory, swap, extensions and downloads; this website cannot guarantee secure erasure or control them. Website code may be cached, but the app does not persist your documents.

Does this validate my digital signature?

No. A signature field or marker triggers a conservative check-only restriction. It is not proof that a signature exists or is valid. In check mode, the original bytes are used without rewriting them. Use your trusted signature validator to check identity, trust, revocation and validity. No certificate, signing key or DSC token is requested.

Which files can I prepare?

Static unsigned PDFs, JPEGs and PNGs. Images become full-page colour images on A4 pages, without cropping. PDFs retain their page dimensions. Password-protected, malformed and oversized inputs are rejected. Forms, signature fields, annotations, PDF scripts, portfolios and embedded files are check-only. PDF document-level bookmarks, tags and metadata are not retained in a new combined file. No OCR, editing, signing or legal validation is performed.

Safety limits: 40 files, 40 MB each, 100 MB total, 200 pages, and images up to 32 megapixels. Modern Chromium browsers are the initial supported target; other browsers need separate validation.
